## Executive Summary

**Recommendation: start with Variant B (the pop-up), and specifically the *residency* form of it — a recurring, ticketed, hidden-door magic-and-cocktails night inside an existing licensed venue.** This exact pattern is proven in the reference ecosystem: LA's "Magic|Bar" pop-up staged a 12-seat, hidden-entrance, rotating-guest-magician cocktail show inside a licensed host venue that sat idle half the week — host supplied the space and liquor infrastructure, magicians supplied the show, no full-time staff **[VERIFIED, 3-0]**. It is the lowest-capital, lowest-legal-load way to test the one thing nobody can verify from a desk: whether enough affluent Caracas customers will pay a premium ticket for close-up magic.

| | Variant A — fixed lounge (30–60 seats) | Variant B — pop-up / residency |
|---|---|---|
| Startup cost | **~$45k–110k** [ILLUSTRATIVE] | **~$6k–18k** [ILLUSTRATIVE] |
| Time to first show | **5–10 months** (full alcohol-license chain + espectáculos permit + showroom build) | **~1–2 months** (partner venue already licensed) |
| Monthly break-even | **~$4,500–6,500** revenue (≈ 55–80 ticketed guests/week) [ILLUSTRATIVE] | **Per-event** — each show independently profitable or not run |
| Legal load | Full bar chain **plus** spectacle permit/tax layer | Mostly offloaded to host venue *(extent [UNVERIFIED] — top gestor question)* |
| Downside if demand is thin | Dead lease + showroom build-out | Cancel the residency, keep the brand and kit |

**Top 5 risks**

1. **Unproven premium demand.** No verified data on affluent-Caracas willingness-to-pay for ticketed magic. The market signal that exists is thin: local magicians earn **$150–400 per show** *(Radio Fe y Alegría, Feb 2024 — single practitioner)* **[WEAK]**, and mentalist Michel Gallero ran his show "Enigmas" Thursdays at Teatro Escena 8, Caracas in May 2024 **[SOURCED — El Diario, Apr 2024]** — proof a ticketed mind-magic show can exist in Caracas, not proof it thrives. Only pop-ups answer this.
2. **Talent single-point-of-failure.** Your partner *is* the product. He gets sick, injured, or burned out → revenue stops. Aggravated by a thin national bench: **~80% of professional magicians have emigrated** *(VOA, per magician "El Mago León")* **[WEAK — single-practitioner estimate]**.
3. **The spectacle-tax/permit layer is real but unquantified.** Venezuela's municipal *impuesto sobre espectáculos públicos* is charged **on the ticket price, paid by the buyer, with the promoter responsible**, at a rate **each municipality sets by ordinance** **[SOURCED — LOCAPTEM/LOPPM summaries]**; Chacao reformed its *Ordenanza de Espectáculos Públicos* on 21 Dec 2023 (Gaceta 9545) **[SOURCED — ordinance exists]** — but **no rate or permit cost survived verification [UNVERIFIED]**. This is the #1 lawyer question before pricing tickets.
4. **Fixed-variant cost stack.** A showroom needs build-out, lighting/sound, and higher rent *on top of* the ~60–70% first-year failure base rate for Caracas venues **[VERIFIED, prior report]**.
5. **Concept theft / shallow imitation.** The format is copyable by any venue that hires a magician for Fridays. Your moat is the *performer's* quality, the drinks craft, and the brand ritual — none of which is legally protectable in practice.

**Total timeline to a real go/no-go decision: ~4–6 months** — incorporate lean, launch the residency, run 12–16 shows, read the numbers.

## 1. The Reference Models — what actually makes them work

### Chicago Magic Lounge (the operational blueprint) **[VERIFIED, 3-0 across all elements]**

- **Purpose-built 7,200 sq ft Art Deco venue, opened Feb 2018**, entered through a **fake laundromat — the hidden/secret entrance is a core branding device**, not decoration.
- **Three stacked revenue spaces:** (1) a **speakeasy performance bar, free entry, nightly 5pm–close, with bartending magicians** — the funnel; (2) the **107-seat Harry Blackstone Cabaret Theater** — ticketed main show; (3) the **43-seat "654 Club" close-up gallery** — the premium inner sanctum.
- **Revenue architecture separates tickets from F&B:** Signature Show tickets **$60–103 + $4.99 fee, tiered by seat location**; **premium tiers bundle post-show access to the 654 Club**; food is optional shareable small plates with **no food-and-drink minimum**.
- **Local heritage as IP:** the 654 Club is named for the "Sam the Bellhop" routine of Chicago magicians Frank Everhart Sr. and Bill Malone. **This does not transfer** — a Caracas concept must build its own mythology (Venezuelan magic history, the SVI lineage, your partner's own story), not borrow Chicago's.

**What transfers to a 30–60 seat Caracas operation:** the *layered access* model (free-ish bar magic → ticketed parlor show → premium inner room), tiered seat pricing, the hidden entrance, tickets-separate-from-F&B. **What doesn't:** the scale (7,200 sq ft, purpose-built), the $60–103 price points (a US benchmark, not a Caracas one), the deep local magic lineage.

### The Magic Castle (the aspiration, not the starting point) **[VERIFIED, 3-0]**

- **Private members' club** monetizing exclusivity and dining rather than tickets: Associate membership **$750/year + $1,500 one-time initiation**, open to non-magicians over 21, **but sponsor-gated** (an existing Academy of Magical Arts member must vouch for you) — the gatekeeping *is* the product.
- **Strict dress code** (suits/dresses; denim, athletic wear, sandals refused at the door) — enforced scarcity and occasion-making.
- Depends on: a wealthy dense membership base, a large building with multiple theaters, and the Academy's institutional IP. **Transfers to Caracas only as a Stage-3 aspiration** — but the *mechanisms* (member/waitlist tiers, sponsor-gating, dress code as ritual) are free to borrow at any scale.

### Magic|Bar, Los Angeles (the actual template for you) **[VERIFIED, 3-0]**

- Magic Castle-community magicians staged a cocktails-and-magic pop-up **inside an existing licensed hospitality venue** (a sushi bar / Woodley Proper) **that sat vacant half the week**.
- **Hidden rolling-wooden-door entrance, just 12 seats**, ~$15 cocktails made by the host's beverage director; **rotating roster of guest magicians** (one or two a week), each doing a tailored close-up act — **no employed performers, no lease, host supplies the liquor infrastructure**.
- *Caveat:* the specific host venue appears closed as of ~Oct 2025 — it validates the **model**, it is no longer a live example **[VERIFIED incl. caveat]**.

**This is your Variant B, already de-risked by someone else's experiment:** small room + hidden door + rotating talent + host venue's license and bar = a magic lounge with almost no capital.

### Branding/IP constraints

"Magic Castle," "Academy of Magical Arts," "Chicago Magic Lounge" are established brands — **don't imitate names or trade dress; build an original Venezuelan brand** (see §7). The *formats* (hidden entrance, close-up gallery, membership) are not protectable and are freely reusable. **[SOURCED/common-sense; neither operates as a franchise system, so there is no licensed path to use their brands]**

## 2. The Venezuelan Magic & Entertainment Context

- **An organized magic community exists:** the **Sociedad Venezolana de Ilusionismo (SVI)**, founded 7 Feb 2001 in Caracas (from informal gatherings since March 1998), federated in FLASOMA — a real, reachable local talent network for a guest-roster strategy **[VERIFIED at medium confidence — supports are the SVI's own site and a magic blog; part of current leadership has emigrated (president based in Doral, USA)]**.
- **But the talent pool is thin and crisis-depressed:** ~**80% of professional magicians have emigrated** *(VOA, magician César "El Mago León" Albornoz, ~20 yrs experience)* and per-show fees run **$150–400** *(Radio Fe y Alegría, Feb 2024)* — both **[WEAK — single-practitioner anecdotes, not surveys]**. Note a source conflict: the $150–400 figure verified against Radio Fe y Alegría but its attribution to the VOA article was **refuted** — treat the band as one working magician's 2024 estimate. Two implications: **(a)** real single-point-of-failure risk, **(b)** guest performers are *cheap* by international standards — a $200 guest slot is affordable inside a single well-sold show.
- **Proof a ticketed mind-magic show can run in Caracas:** mentalist **Michel Gallero** (Caracas-born, marketed as Latin America's #1 mentalist) staged **"Enigmas" on Thursdays in May 2024 at Teatro Escena 8** **[SOURCED — El Diario, 20 Apr 2024]**. Existence proof, not a success metric — no attendance/economics published **[UNVERIFIED]**.
- **Experiential nightlife precedent:** Caracas already supports reservation-gated speakeasies (6310 Speakeasy) **[SOURCED, prior report]** and premium bottle-service nightlife (e.g., $85–215/bottle at ECO) **[SOURCED — El Diario, Feb 2024]** — evidence of an affluent, dollarized segment that pays for exclusivity, though **not** direct evidence it will buy magic tickets.
- **What could not be verified:** any currently-operating magic bar/dinner-theater in Caracas 2024–2026 (none found — you would likely be first, which cuts both ways), and quantified premium-experience demand **[UNVERIFIED]**.

## 3. The Performance/Entertainment Regulatory Overlay

**Headline honesty: this is the report's weakest-sourced section. NO claim about Venezuelan spectacle permits, rates, or club legality survived verification.** What follows is the structure per sourced law summaries, with every number flagged.

- **The tax exists and its mechanics are clear:** the municipal ***impuesto sobre espectáculos públicos*** is levied **on each ticket/boleto**, **paid by the buyer at purchase**, with the **promoter/empresario responsible** for collection, and the **rate set by each municipality's own ordinance** (it may vary by spectacle type) **[SOURCED — LOPPM/LOCAPTEM summaries: Sabatrif, rodriguezc.com, CEPAL]**. It stacks *on top of* the economic-activities tax on your gross (4.75% for alcohol service in Chacao, prior report).
- **Chacao reformed its *Ordenanza de Espectáculos Públicos* on 21 Dec 2023** (Gaceta Municipal 9545 — official PDF exists on rentas.chacao.gob.ve but was not extractable through the proxy) and operates a **"Solicitud de Permiso de Espectáculo Público"** trámite (incl. an exoneration variant) on the same portal as the liquor licenses **[SOURCED — pages exist; contents/rates [UNVERIFIED]]**.
- **The rate and permit cost: [UNVERIFIED].** Get the Gaceta 9545 text from a gestor before pricing tickets — a 10%-of-boletería-class tax would materially change the fixed variant's math.
- **IVA on admissions: [UNVERIFIED]** — whether your tickets carry 16% IVA, the spectacle tax, or both is a tax-advisor question.
- **A structural dodge worth pricing (lawyer question, not advice):** if the ticket is legally framed as a **cover/consumo mínimo or a reservation with included cocktails** rather than *boletería* for a *espectáculo público*, the spectacle-tax layer may not attach — this is exactly how many venue residencies operate, and exactly the kind of thing only a Caracas tax lawyer can bless **[ILLUSTRATIVE — verify]**.
- **SACVEN/AVINPRO:** obligations extend to venues with live performance + recorded music; tariff schedules were "en revisión" and a live-magic-show tariff is **[UNVERIFIED]** (prior report). A magic act itself isn't a musical work, but your soundtrack and any live music are.
- **Private members'-club legality:** whether a Magic-Castle-style private club changes the alcohol or spectacle licensing burden in Venezuela is **[UNVERIFIED]** — civil associations (*asociaciones civiles*) exist as a form, but no source addressed their interaction with *expendio* licensing. Lawyer question.
- **Fire/occupancy for an audience:** bomberos permitting exists (prior report); whether a seated performance audience triggers stricter assembly rules at 30–60 seats is **[UNVERIFIED]**.
- **Pop-up variant:** the *Expendio Eventual* and per-event spectacle permits exist (prior report; Chacao portal) **[SOURCED]**, but **whether a licensed host venue's permits fully cover your ticketed show inside it is [UNVERIFIED] — this is the single most important legal question for Variant B.** The safest opening posture: the **host venue is the promoter/seller of record** (tickets/covers run through their till; you're a contracted production), which mirrors the verified Magic|Bar structure.

## 4. Concept & Experience Design (2 people, 30–60 seats)

**Recommended format: the hybrid, scaled from Chicago's three layers to two.**

1. **The bar = the stage.** Your partner's dual skill is the concept: **the cocktailier is the magician**. Card work between builds, a trick served *with* the drink. Chicago staffs "bartending magicians" nightly **[VERIFIED]** — you have one in the founding team. This layer works even on non-show nights and makes the drinks program itself the differentiator.
2. **The parlor set.** 1–2 seated close-up/parlor shows a night (25–40 min each) on show nights, ticket-gated, 20–40 seats. Small parlor magic needs no stage rig — lighting and sightlines matter more than sound **[ILLUSTRATIVE]**.
3. Later, a **"654-style" inner ritual** — a premium tier (front table, post-show private set, the "member's pour") that costs you nothing and creates the ladder Chicago monetizes **[VERIFIED pattern]**.

**Sustainable cadence for one magician [ILLUSTRATIVE, but anchored to how working close-up magicians tour]:** 2–3 show nights/week × 2 sets is sustainable; 5+ nights of hosting *plus* performing *plus* prep is not — protect him like the asset he is. Extend the calendar with **guest magicians as per-event contractors** (SVI network, $150–400/show local rate **[WEAK]**) — preserving the no-payroll / no-*inamovilidad* structure (prior report **[VERIFIED]**), exactly as Magic|Bar ran its rotating roster **[VERIFIED]**.

**Hidden-entrance branding vs. Venezuelan reality:** the secret door is marketing, not legal cover. The venue (or host venue) must be fully permitted and inspectable — a "hidden" bar that is *administratively* hidden is a shutdown waiting to happen in Venezuela's inspection culture (prior report). Hide the entrance, never the paperwork. And the ambulatory-sales prohibition (prior report **[SOURCED]**) means the pop-up sells alcohol only under a licensed host or an *Expendio Eventual* — never free-standing.

Versus the plain micro-bar (break-even ~15 guests/night), the lounge needs **fewer but higher-paying** guests — a better fit for a thin-but-affluent market *if* the demand exists. Which is the whole question, which is why B goes first.

## 6. Talent, Programming & Scaling Without Staff

- **Guest roster via the SVI network** **[VERIFIED the institution exists]** — per-event contractor agreements (invoiced services, never salaried) preserve the no-payroll/no-*inamovilidad* advantage **[VERIFIED, prior report]**. At **$150–400/show [WEAK]**, one strong guest is affordable inside a single sold-out night; the diaspora (80% emigrated **[WEAK]**) is also a *feature* for special events — a returning Venezuelan name is a marketable homecoming show.
- **Mitigating the single-magician SPOF:** (1) build the brand around the *house* and its ritual, not solely the performer's name; (2) train the residency format so a guest can carry a night; (3) keep 2–3 SVI regulars warm; (4) the business-lead partner hosts/MCs so the show has a second live pillar; (5) insure the performer's hands the way a restaurant insures its kitchen — figuratively at minimum: no-show contingency plan per booked night.
- **Programming beyond shows (all high-margin, no staff):** magic workshops/masterclasses (daytime revenue), corporate team events, kids-free adult "midnight séance" premium nights, cocktail-and-card-trick classes taught by the same person who is both — the dual skill is a programming goldmine.

## 7. Branding, Positioning & Marketing (the "face" role)

- **Build an original Venezuelan mythology** — do not clone the Castle/Chicago names or dress **[IP hygiene]**. Raw material: Venezuela's own illusionism lineage (SVI, 1998→2001 **[VERIFIED-medium]**), local legends and lore, your partner's biography. Chicago's lesson is that *local heritage embedded in the brand* is the moat **[VERIFIED — the 654 story]**.
- **Scarcity mechanics on ~$0** (all proven by the reference models): hidden entrance + password reveal on show day; 12–40 seats *because intimacy is the product* (Magic|Bar's 12 seats **[VERIFIED]**); reservation-only; a visible waitlist; premium inner-tier ritual (Chicago's 654 bundling **[VERIFIED]**); later, sponsor-gated "membership" à la Castle ($750/$1,500 US anchors **[VERIFIED]** — a Caracas tier might be $15–30/mo **[ILLUSTRATIVE]**).
- **The face-of-operation role is a real job:** you are the MC, the door, the Instagram voice, the press contact, and the corporate-sales channel. Founder-led accounts outperform venue accounts; document every show cinematically (magic is the most shareable bar content that exists — reactions *are* the reel).
- **Positioning for a dollarized-but-unequal market:** price as an **occasion** ($20–35 all-in experience vs. a $40 restaurant dinner **[SOURCED benchmark]**), not as nightly entertainment. Target celebrations, dates, corporate hosting — the segments already paying $85–215 for bottle service **[SOURCED]**.

## 9. Sequencing & Recommendation

**Stage 0 (weeks 0–6) — legal foundation.** Incorporate the C.A.; RIF; structure as an **entertainment-production + bar-service company** (services first, alcohol later); gestor answers the two blocking questions: *(a)* does performing ticketed shows inside a licensed host venue require any permit of ours? *(b)* Chacao/El Hatillo spectacle-tax rate and whether cover-with-included-drinks avoids boletería treatment.

**Stage 1 (months 1–4) — the residency (Variant B).** One partner venue (idle nights, good room, existing licenses — the Magic|Bar template **[VERIFIED]**); host is seller of record; launch the hidden-door brand + founder-led Instagram; 1 night/week → 2; guest magician every 2nd–3rd show via SVI; sell private/corporate events off the back of it. **Capital: $6k–18k.**

**Stage 2 (months 4–6) — read the data.** Sell-through, waitlist depth, repeat rate, corporate pipeline against the §5 tripwires. **This decision is made with receipts, not optimism.**

**Stage 3 (months 6–16, only on green) — the fixed lounge (Variant A).** Now the full chain from the prior reports applies, *plus* the spectacle permit layered on: incorporation → RIF → zoned premises (*conformidad de uso* before signing) → patente-with-alcohol → liquor license → sanitary/bomberos/SACVEN-AVINPRO → **espectáculos públicos permit** (sequence vs. the liquor license **[UNVERIFIED]** — likely parallel after patente; confirm). Build the two-layer room (magic bar + parlor). The residency continues *inside* your own venue.

**Stage 4 (year 2+, optional) — the membership ritual.** Sponsor-gated tier, members' nights, the inner room — the Magic Castle mechanics **[VERIFIED]** at Caracas scale, only once there's a community to gate.
