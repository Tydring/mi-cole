## Open choices
- Tagline: "Lo que pasa en el aula, llega a la casa." is a proposal, not settled copy. Swap it for anything you prefer.
- End line: shows no phone number or email on purpose (a video gets forwarded for years; a number can change). Can add the WhatsApp number if you want.
- Background: light (paper) by default. A dark version (slate) is a 1-line change.

## Sound (after picture lock, not before)
- No sfx folder or music in the project yet. Prototype will be silent.
- Proposed cues once the picture is locked: soft pop (dot, 0.0s), quick whoosh (line, 1.0s), soft thud (cap lands, 3.0s), light tick on each word of the tagline.
- Sources must be generated or licensed. The piece has to work muted (most WhatsApp and Instagram viewing is muted), and it does: all the meaning is on screen.
