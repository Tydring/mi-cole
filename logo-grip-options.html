How hard the cap grips the C

The cap now sits in front of the C and wears it: the top of the letter becomes the crown of the cap, and the cut runs parallel to the curve of the C. Same cap and tilt in all three. Only the depth changes.

Light grip

The cap covers the top 14% of the C. The cut runs parallel to the curve of the letter.

Medium grip (my pick)

The cap covers the top 20% of the C. Clearly worn, the C still reads at a glance.

Deep grip

The cap covers the top 26% of the C. Pulled down low, the most hat, the least C.
