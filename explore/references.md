# miCole homepage: references (Phase 2, step 2a)

From memory of well-known sites, not a live browse. Each note says what to take and what to leave. The rule: take the strongest ideas and combine them into one coherent direction. Never copy one site, and never average them.

1. **Linear (linear.app)**. Take: one huge, confident headline and a lot of empty space; the product shown as one precise object, not a collage. Leave: the dark tech gradients and glow; that reads "developer tool", not "colegio".
2. **Stripe Press / editorial magazine layouts**. Take: big serif-free type set like a magazine, numbered sections, text a director can read like a letter. Leave: long-form density; our reader is on a phone between meetings.
3. **Brightwheel / Seesaw (school apps, US)**. Take: the warmth of showing a real parent moment (a photo of the day arriving on a phone). Leave: primary colors, cartoon kids, stock-photo smiles, everything that reads "generic edtech".
4. **Paper school objects, Venezuela**. The "cuaderno de comunicaciones", the stapled circular, the chalkboard, the "morral" where circulares get lost. Take: the material and the vocabulary, drawn simply. Leave: nostalgia and texture overload; it has to load fast on a mid-range Android phone.
5. **Real assets**. The locked logo (brand/final/), the palette (Pizarra, Papel, Mango, Guayaba, Salvia, Tiza, Pizarra noche), Bricolage Grotesque 700 for headings, system font for body, and the motion of the logo video (a dot that becomes a thread, a cap that lands).

The one idea every direction has to serve: **the link between the classroom and the home** (lo que pasa en el aula, llega a la casa), without anyone giving out a personal WhatsApp.
